# Voice & Video Calls

Make high-quality calls with AMIK CHAT.

## Voice Call

1. Open contact
2. Click  **Call** icon
3. Wait for answer

## Video Call

1. Open contact
2. Click  **Video** icon
3. Enable camera

## During Call

-  **Mute**: Mute your mic
-  **Camera**: Toggle camera on/off
-  **Screen Share**: Share your screen
-  **Speaker**: Switch audio output

## Call Quality

For best quality:
- Use Wi-Fi
- Close other apps
- Ensure good lighting (video)
