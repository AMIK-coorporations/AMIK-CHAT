# Account Setup

Create your AMIK CHAT account in minutes.

## Sign Up

1. Visit [amikchat.site](https://amikchat.site)
2. Click **Sign Up**
3. Enter your information:
   - Email address
   - Password (8+ characters)
   - Display name
4. Click **Create Account**

## Email Verification

1. Check your email inbox
2. Click the verification link
3. Your account is ready!
