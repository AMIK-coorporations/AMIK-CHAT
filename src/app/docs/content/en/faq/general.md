# FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

## Account

**Q: Forgot password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page.

**Q: Change email?**
A: Go to Settings > Profile > Email

## Messaging

**Q: Can I unsend messages?**
A: Yes, within 1 hour of sending.

**Q: Message limits?**
A: No limits on text. Files up to 100MB.

## Calls

**Q: Call quality poor?**
A: Check your internet connection. Use Wi-Fi.

**Q: Can I record calls?**
A: Not currently available.
