# Scan QR Code

Add contacts instantly by scanning their QR code.

## How to Scan

1. Open a contact's QR code
2. In AMIK: Click **+** > **Scan QR**
3. Point camera at the QR code
4. Contact added automatically!

## Tips

- Ensure good lighting
- Hold steady
- QR should fill the frame
